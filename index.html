DUTY BRIEFING · 值勤簡報

You are tonight's duty officer.你是今晚的值班軍官。

26 September 1983, just after midnight. An underground command post south of Moscow, code name Serpukhov-15. Your screens are wired to the Oko early-warning satellites. 1983 年 9 月 26 日，午夜過後。莫斯科南方的地下指揮所，代號 Серпухов-15。你的螢幕連著 Око 預警衛星網。

Your job is one thing only: decide whether what is on the screen is real, and whether to report it. 你的職責只有一件事：判斷螢幕上的東西是不是真的，然後決定要不要上報。

STANDING DOCTRINE · 當時的準則

Soviet doctrine is launch on warning. Once your report goes up the chain, the counter-strike sequence begins. 蘇聯採取「預警即發射」。你的報告一旦沿指揮鏈上傳，反擊程序就會啟動。

And if the duty officer never confirms the warning, the counter-strike sequence proceeds under doctrine anyway. 若值班軍官最終未能協助確認預警狀態，將依準則直接啟動核反擊。

VERIFICATION · 查證

Checks open once all launches have been plotted, and may be run as many times as you like. Every check costs time. Headquarters will not wait forever. 所有發射標定完成後，查證管道才會開放，之後次數不限。但每一次查證都要花時間，而指揮部不會一直等下去。

Three checks are available:
RECHECK RADAR — order another ground-radar pass (12s)
CHECK FAULT LOG — pull the satellite fault record (10s)
HOLD HQ — tell headquarters you are still verifying (6s) 可用的查證有三種：
RECHECK RADAR　要求地面雷達複查（12 秒）
CHECK FAULT LOG　調閱衛星故障紀錄（10 秒）
HOLD HQ　回覆指揮部仍在查證（6 秒）
