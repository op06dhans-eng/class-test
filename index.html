You are tonight's duty officer.你是今晚的值班軍官。

26 September 1983, just after midnight. An underground command post south of Moscow, code name Serpukhov-15. Your screens are wired to the Oko early-warning satellites. 1983 年 9 月 26 日，午夜過後。莫斯科南方的地下指揮所，代號 Серпухов-15。你的螢幕連著 Око 預警衛星網。

Your job is one thing only: decide whether what is on the screen is real, and whether to report it. 你的職責只有一件事：判斷螢幕上的東西是不是真的，然後決定要不要上報。

STANDING DOCTRINE · 當時的準則

Soviet doctrine is launch on warning. Once your report goes up the chain, the counter-strike sequence begins. 蘇聯採取「預警即發射」。你的報告一旦沿指揮鏈上傳，反擊程序就會啟動。

You may run checks, as many times as you like. Every check costs time. Headquarters will not wait forever. 你可以查證，次數不限。但每一次查證都要花時間，而指揮部不會一直等下去。
